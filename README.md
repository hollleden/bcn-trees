# 🌳 BCN Trees — Interactive Map of Barcelona's Notable Trees
An interactive map of all 208 trees from Barcelona's official catalogue of *[Arbres d'interès local](https://www.barcelona.cat/ca/que-pots-fer-a-bcn/parcs-i-jardins/arbres-interes-local)* — trees, palms, and shrubs protected by the city for their age, rarity, or historical value. Click any marker to see the species, its story, and how to get there.

## What it does

- Shows all 208 catalogued trees on a map with clustering — zoom in to see individual markers
- Every tree has a detail card: photo, Latin name, common name in Russian and Catalan, full description, and characteristics (crown size, trunk perimeter, catalogue number, year of registration)
- Filter by tree type (palms, conifers, flowering, fruit, deciduous), district, ownership, age, size, and whether you've already visited
- Full-text search across every field — species, neighbourhood, address, year
- Build a walking route to any tree via Google Maps, or open the official city page
- Works in two languages: Russian and Spanish — switch without reload
- Runs entirely from a single HTML file, no backend

## Why

Barcelona has one of the most detailed tree catalogues in Europe, but it lives buried in a PDF on a government website. This project makes that catalogue walkable — you can see which trees are near you right now, what makes each one special, and plan a route to the ones you want to visit.

Some trees here are older than the city's most famous buildings. A few survived wars. One grew inside a courtyard that used to be a dairy. The map is a way to meet them.

Built as a personal project — not an official city product.

## Who it's for

- Anyone walking around Barcelona who wants to know what they're looking at
- Tree lovers and botanists curious about Mediterranean and exotic species
- Travellers looking for something other than the usual sights
- Locals who want to discover a side of their city they've never noticed

## Data sources

All tree data comes from the public catalogue *[Arbres d'interès local](https://www.barcelona.cat/ca/que-pots-fer-a-bcn/parcs-i-jardins/arbres-interes-local)* on barcelona.cat — photos, descriptions, coordinates, and registration details. Each card links back to the official page it came from.
